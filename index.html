      Module 2 of 6 · Our Chancellor

      Meet your Chancellor.

      Ninth Chancellor · since July 1, 2020 Daniel Diermeier

      An internationally renowned scholar of political science and managerial leadership. Born in Berlin, a first-generation college graduate, and before Vanderbilt, provost of the University of Chicago.

      More about the Chancellor
      • Before Vanderbilt: provost and dean of the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago, and a longtime professor at Stanford and at Northwestern’s Kellogg School of Management.
      • Chosen by the Vanderbilt Board of Trust after an extensive worldwide search.
      • In his first years: guided Vanderbilt through the COVID-19 pandemic, and launched Destination Vanderbilt, to recruit renowned faculty, and the Vanderbilt Project on Unity and American Democracy.
      • Since: research passes $1 billion, the Vandy United campaign for student-athletes, and a commitment to free expression and civil discourse.
