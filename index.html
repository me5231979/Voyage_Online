    The mission · Next: lesson 1, welcome to the Voyage

    The mission

    Define the great university of the 21st century, and be it.

    What you will learnThe vision, how we operate, where we work, and your part in it.

    Why it mattersEvery role at Vanderbilt connects to this mission, including yours, starting today.

    How we operate

    Vanderbilt operates at uncommon speed, agility, and scale. Three areas of focus.

    Areas of focus
    1Exceptional Core Operations

    With speed, agility and scale, Vanderbilt will distinguish itself with innovative and impactful capabilities to ensure that we have the talent, resources and reputation necessary to deliver on our vision.

    2Bold Strategic Initiatives

    With “exceptional core operations” as our fuel, Vanderbilt will pursue increasingly bold initiatives that enable and enhance the reach and impact of our education and research mission.

    3Values Leadership

    Through strategic advocacy efforts and modeling the role of an essential research university, Vanderbilt will stimulate industry change and combat existential threats to our education and research mission.

    Read the Chancellor’s vision, “Daring to Grow,” at vanderbilt.edu

    As we grow
    One Vanderbilt, five cities

    Vanderbilt now works from five cities, with Nashville as home. As we grow, the same mission, beliefs, and ways of working travel with us, wherever you sit.

    • Nashville
    • Chattanooga
    • New York City
    • West Palm Beach
    • San Francisco

    Read about all five campuses at vanderbilt.edu

    Your part

    Whatever your role, the mission is achieved through the work you do. From today, you help Vanderbilt be it.

    • Know it. Learn the vision and what it asks of your team.
    • Connect your work to it. Ask how what you do this week moves it forward.
    • Stay in touch with your manager. Priorities, projects, and how you are settling in.

    Lesson 2 · Our leadership

    Visionary leadership.

    What you will learnWho leads Vanderbilt, and what they are building.

    Why it mattersKnowing where the university is headed helps you see where your work fits.

    Chancellor Daniel Diermeier

    Born in Berlin, Germany, Daniel Diermeier is a first-generation college graduate who embodies Vanderbilt’s commitment to making an elite education accessible to all. His leadership steered the safe return of students to campus during the COVID-19 pandemic.

    Under his tenure, and in line with the motto Crescere aude, “dare to grow,” Vanderbilt has passed $1 billion in research expenditures, launched a successful capital campaign, and reaffirmed its commitment to free expression and civil discourse.

    The vice chancellors

    The Chancellor’s leadership team brings deep experience from across higher education. Each vice chancellor guides a part of the university toward the mission: academic affairs, financial operations, and the culture and belonging of our staff, among others. Together they keep Vanderbilt at the front of higher education, and make sure students have what they need to succeed.

    Lesson 5 · Crescere aude

    A motto, and a mindset.

    What you will learnWhat Crescere aude means, and how daring to grow fits your first year here.

    Why it mattersGrowth is how we reach our full potential, and at Vanderbilt nobody is expected to grow alone.

    The motto

    Crescere aude. Latin for “dare to grow.”

    It is more than a motto on a seal. It is a mindset: the choice to keep learning, to take on the stretch, and to help the people around you do the same.

    1Adopt a growth mindset.

    Ability grows with effort. A mistake is information, not a verdict.

    2Use what is here.

    Mentors, programs, courses, and your manager. Ask for them.

    3Grow together.

    Work with your teammates. Growth is a team sport here.
